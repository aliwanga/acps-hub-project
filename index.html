Questions and answers

How do I make alerts ring on my phone?

1) Install the app (Install app button, or Add to Home Screen on iPhone). 2) Settings, then Enable notifications and Allow. 3) On Android open phone Settings, Apps, ACPS HUB (or Chrome), Notifications, and set it to Alert/High importance with sound. 4) While the app is open it rings with a loud alarm until you dismiss it. 5) For a guaranteed ring even when the app is closed, tap the 🔔 button on a reminder or assignment and open the file to add it to your phone's calendar alarm.

I forgot my password

On the login screen type your email and tap Forgot password. We email you a secure link. Open it, and you will be asked to create a new password. Check your spam folder if you cannot find it.

Can I use it on another phone or laptop?

Yes. Log in with the same email and password and your data appears. It syncs automatically.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Changes are saved on your device and sync when you are back online.

How is my GPA calculated?

70+ = A (4), 60-69 = B (3), 50-59 = C (2), 40-49 = D (1), below 40 = E (0), weighted by credit hours. Check this against your university's rules.

How do I install the app?

Android or computer: tap Install app at the top. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Your data is stored under your own account.

Your data is saved to your account and synced across your devices.
